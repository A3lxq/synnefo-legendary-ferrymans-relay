# Case File 6: Ferryman's Relay

The audit log from the Annex named a service Priya Okonkwo-Reyes had
flagged months before she vanished: an in-house relay tool, built by
Halcyon's own infrastructure team for moving data between departments
that were never supposed to talk to each other directly.

Her note says she got as far as the relay's internal client tool and
its login portal — and that everything past that point was "built by
people who assumed nobody outside the company would ever see the
binary." She was right that nobody outside the company would see it.
She was wrong that it would matter.

Her note: "The front door has a lock. Everything behind it assumes you
already work here."
